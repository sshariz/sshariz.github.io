My Journey

I’ve been working with data for over 6 years now across a few disciplines: Data Science and Visualization, Data Analysis, Analytics Engineering, and Data Engineering. This started in 2019 when I won a scholarship for a coding program that aimed to make tech more inclusive in youth.

From there I attended UC Berkeley, where I double majored in both Data Science & Business Administration. I was driven to pursue simultaneous degrees because I wanted to develop the analytical mindset that would allow me to understand and solve complex technical problems, while having the business perspective to know why those solutions matter.

I currently work at an Enterprise Software Platform based in NYC with presence in London and Hong Kong.
