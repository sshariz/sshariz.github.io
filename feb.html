Formula SAE Business Lead

Formula Electric at Berkeley (FEB) is an organization of the Society of Automotive Engineers & 501c3 non-profit that competes in Formula SAE - an annual collegiate competition in which student teams from all around the world design, build, and race formula-style race cars.

As a Business Lead, I Co-Directed 80+ students alongside FEB's Autonomous, Mechanical, & Electrical Engineering Leads in the development of our 3rd electric race car.

Problem Statement

The estimated cost to compete & build an FSAE electric vehicle from scratch is around $75,000 to $115,000. This budget comprises the Electronics, LV systems, Accumulator (Battery Pack), Powertrain, Chassis, Aerodynamics, & Logistical costs to get to Michigan.

FEB depends on crucial support from our sponsors. Funding to buy materials, in-kind donations (parts, services, facility access), and intellectual collaboration from our mentors all drive our success in competition.
